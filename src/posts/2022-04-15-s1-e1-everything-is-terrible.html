Welcome to the Bleak Theology Podcast.

It is Good Friday and to coincide with this most dreadful day of the Christian year, I am sharing my first episode. I have been writing bleak theology for a long time, but with the world as it is these days, it seemed that such a podcast might be well received.

The first episode is a primer or rather, an orientation of what’s in store. I am not aware of any podcasts like this one, which is one of the reasons why I’ve started it. I hope that you will find it meaningful, especially on this second day of the Triduum.

All episodes will be available on the Podcast page, as well as on your preferred podcast app. The podcast will be available on Apple Podcast, iHeartRadio, Pandora, and Overcast in the very near future.

If you would please rate and review the podcast, I would greatly appreciate it and I encourage you to subscribe and share the podcast with others. If you have questions or comments, feel free to reach out on the podcast page.

The world doesn’t need another religion podcast, but I started one, anyway.

COVID, war, racism, existential angst, climate collapse, economic injustice, genocide, fascism, sexism, racial injustice, trans- and homophobia, general malaise and anxiety, etc., etc. Things look pretty bleak, right now. Let’s talk about that.

If you’re into Christianity, existential crises, and the seemingly insurmountable abyss between the human and the divine, then this is your podcast.

First episode drops, unsurprisingly, on Good Friday.

S1 E2 Exvangelicals, Deconstruction, and World's End – The Bleak Theology Podcast

Has the "biblical worldview" always existed? Can the exvangelical escape evangelicalism? Why does "deconstruction" sound so familiar? So many questions. My answers will probably trouble you. But it wouldn’t be bleak theology if Plato, Nietzsche, and Derrida didn’t show up, right? And there’s even some Marx, just for fun. I can’t say that it’s postmodern Marxism, but maybe?

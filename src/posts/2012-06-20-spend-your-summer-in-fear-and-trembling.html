This summer, I will be teaching a short four-week course on how to think theologically and I'll be using Søren Kierkegaard's Fear and Trembling as the basic text. Yes, it does seem like a your-chocolate-in-my-peanut-butter/your-peanut-butter-in-my-chocolate sort of debacle, but I believe it will work. 

I was pleased with the response and believe there's wider interest than just my immediate dinner church, so I'll be posting synopses and resources here on Bleak Theology, as we go along.
